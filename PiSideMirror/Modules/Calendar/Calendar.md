### Calendar

##### Linking Apple Calendar
1. Login to your [Apple Account](account.apple.com)
2. Select `Sign-In & Security` on the left.
3. Select `App-Specific Passwords` towards the bottom.
4. Click `Generate an app-specific password`
5. Name the password (i.e. Smart Mirror)
6. Copy the new password and input your login into `~/PiSideMirror/Modules/Calendar/config.json`
